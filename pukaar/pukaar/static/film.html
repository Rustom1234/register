What is real here, and what is not

The routing, the dispatch waves, the kit ledger, the emergency gate and the privacy purge are production code, and the map is a real survey. The responders are simulated, and the clock runs at twelve times normal speed — which is why the rider crosses the zone in half a minute. No NGO is running this yet. Nobody has been served by it.
