Pukaar · पुकार · “the call”

The person who stops
finally has somewhere to turn.

A man sits under a Delhi flyover, his foot wrapped in a bandage that's bleeding through. Two hundred rupees of antiseptic and fresh dressing would change his week. Hundreds walk past — not because nobody cares, but because there is nothing to plug that moment of caring into. Pukaar is that plug.

How it works

01 · WITNESS
One WhatsApp message
Pin + a line of text, a photo, or a voice note — in English, Hinglish, or हिन्दी. Four steps, under ninety seconds.
02 · AGENT
Structured, never trusted
An AI layer extracts category and urgency into strict schemas. Emergencies exit to 112 before any model runs.
03 · ORDER
A kit, not a guess
Medical / food / shelter kit orders with code-enforced caution: uncertainty always raises the clinical flag.
04 · RESPONDER
Nearest trusted human
GoodSAM-style parallel offers to vetted NGO responders; first accept wins; a human coordinator is the terminal rung.
05 · CLOSED LOOP
Outcome, always
Served, escalated to care, not found, or declined — logged, and the witness hears back: “someone reached them.”

What makes it different

  • No cameras. No database of the poor. The obvious AI here — scanning streets for homeless people — is a documented failure (San Jose shut theirs down) and a documented danger (location data about the poor gets reused for evictions and begging-law enforcement in India). Pukaar's sensor is a witness who chose to stop.
  • Privacy by architecture, not policy. No identities of street residents, photos deleted at case close, exact locations nulled 7 days on from a closed case (an open case keeps its pin only until it is served), closed cases aggregated to coarse cells at 90 days along with a sweep of reports that never became a case — enforced by a purge job, verified by tests.
  • The model never speaks in the safety path. Every emergency and policy sentence is a fixed, audited string. The test suite fails the build if the 112 gate misses one emergency phrasing.
  • Provenance on every claim. Witness statements and agent inferences are HMAC-signed; responder observations carry provenance labels in the audit log — an AI guess can never impersonate a human's word.
  • Pre-registered kill criteria. Verified-need rate, acceptance, cost per person served, backlog — live on a dashboard. If Pukaar stops earning its numbers, the dashboard says so.

Honest limits

  • This demo simulates the WhatsApp transport and any responder a human hasn't taken over — the standalone /responder app lets a real person play a responder against the live pipeline; the pipeline, gate, dispatch, retention, and provenance are the production code paths.
  • Voice notes flow as transcripts; real speech-to-text (Sarvam) is a pilot task.
  • Sim outcome rates are plausible fictions. The pilot's kill-criteria numbers must come from the street, and we commit to publishing them either way.

Where this goes

One Delhi zone, one NGO partner, 90 days, pre-registered metrics. Then the rails that already exist at scale — checkout micro-donations that fund 200,000 meals a day, and quick-commerce fleets that already run ambulances — plugged into the loop nobody has built: witness → verify → deliver → care.
