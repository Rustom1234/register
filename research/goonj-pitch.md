# 5-line pitch to Goonj — Version B (Signal Loop)

1. Last month I walked past a homeless man whose wrapped foot was bleeding through the bandage — ₹200 of antiseptic, dressings and a raincoat would have changed his week, but there was nothing for a passerby like me to plug that moment into.
2. I'm building that plug: a WhatsApp line where anyone who sees someone in need drops a pin in 90 seconds, and a trusted outreach worker verifies and responds — no cameras, no lists of the poor, pins that expire in 72 hours.
3. Goonj is the response half: a ~₹300 kit built with your material and Rahat-style assembly — wound-care basics, ORS, socks, seasonal protection, packed in a Goonj-cloth bag — reaching people through an outreach worker's hands, never dropped anonymously from a delivery bike.
4. It extends what Odha Do Zindagi already does each winter into a year-round, need-triggered flow, and every kit closes with a logged outcome — accepted, refused, escalated to medical care — so your material generates evidence, not just relief.
5. The ask: co-design kit v1 with me, assemble the first 200 at your Delhi center, and introduce me to one outreach partner you trust — I'm funding the 90-day pilot myself, and the data comes back to you.
