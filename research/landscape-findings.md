# Landscape findings — what already exists

Researched 2026-07-24 via four parallel web sweeps (~65 searches): detection tech, fulfilment rails, open source & apps, legal/ethics. Sources linked inline; thinly-sourced claims marked (unverified).

## The one-paragraph answer

**No product, pilot, government program, or open-source project anywhere does camera → detect need → deliver supplies end to end.** Every layer exists separately: cities have pointed AI at homelessness (and retreated), platforms run charitable delivery rails (to fixed addresses), citizens report people in need through apps (and humans respond), NGOs mass-produce aid kits. Nobody has closed the loop — that loop is the genuinely novel part of the concept. The camera layer specifically is unprecedented *for medical-need inference*, and it's unprecedented for good reasons: the one city that tried the nearest thing shut that component down, and the accuracy, legal, and ethical evidence against it is consistent across every jurisdiction researched.

## 1. Detection: AI on street cameras

**San Jose, CA — the defining precedent, and it failed.** From Dec 2023 the city drove a camera-rigged sedan through district streets, feeding footage to four vendors' AI to detect lived-in cars/RVs (encampment detection to follow). Accuracy: **10–15% on lived-in cars**, 70–75% on RVs. National backlash followed the Guardian's Mar 2024 reveal (ACLU: "systems of mass surveillance supercharge discrimination"); the data-use policy barred law-enforcement use, but police could still request footage. In the city's Sept 2025 results, potholes hit **97%** detection and illegal dumping 88% — and **graffiti and encampment detection were removed from the pilot, citing "capacity limitations and privacy concerns."** The road-hazard pilot continues; the homelessness component is effectively dead. [Governing](https://www.governing.com/urban/san-jose-is-using-ai-to-detect-homeless-camps-will-it-work) · [City results, Sept 2025](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/Home/Components/News/News/6926/4699) · [AIAAIC entry](https://www.aiaaic.org/aiaaic-repository/ai-algorithmic-and-automation-incidents/san-jose-homeless-detection-ai-sparks-privacy-inequality-fears)

**Lucknow "women in distress" cameras — the Indian cautionary tale.** UP Police's ₹195-crore Safe City project (announced Jan 2021) promised AI reading women's facial expressions to auto-alert police. Investigations found high false positives, non-functional cameras, and no evidence of effectiveness; experts called expression-reading "asinine." The only Indian precedent of CCTV "distress inference," and it failed. [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/984704/smart-cameras-to-be-set-up-in-lucknow-to-track-facial-expression-of-women-in-distress-alert-police) · [Pulitzer Center](https://pulitzercenter.org/stories/watched-unprotected-how-lucknows-safe-city-project-fails-women)

**San Diego SDHEART — the most mature "detect → route aid" analog.** NSF-funded ($450k) SDSU GeoAI project: ResNet-50/Faster R-CNN over 0.15–0.6 m aerial imagery + street-level archives + city data, producing a near-real-time encampment dashboard used to route outreach, hygiene stations, and street medicine. Active 2025–26; privacy concerns raised. Aerial and area-level — deliberately not person-level CCTV. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2026/04/21/san-diego-ai-homelessness-map-sdsu) · [sdheart.sdsu.edu](https://sdheart.sdsu.edu/)

**Where camera-AI on vulnerable people is *accepted*: rescue framing with human dispatch.** Seoul runs AI on ~900 CCTV across 17 Han River bridges (since 2021), predicting jump attempts and dispatching rescue — ~1,270 dispatches in 2025, 99% survival reported May 2026. Stockholm's metro system has peer-reviewed evidence of reduced suicides from AI video surveillance (BMC Public Health 2026). Emergency response with a documented rescue outcome is politically viable; population monitoring is not. [Seoul](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-using-ai-based-cctv-to-prevent-suicide-attempts-on-bridges/) · [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/southkorea/society/20260515/at-seouls-bridge-control-center-ai-helps-stop-99-of-suicide-attempts) · [Stockholm study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12889-026-28358-1)

**"Fallen person" detection is a purchasable commercial feature** — Hikvision ships it natively; CVEDIA, Scylla, IncoreSoft, IntelliVision sell modules; practitioners treat "man down" analytics as niche with reliability caveats, mostly indoor/facility deployments. India-relevant: **Videonetics** has demonstrated "person collapsing" detection aimed at Indian Smart City projects, and its VMS already runs Andhra Pradesh's statewide CCTV360 (~15,000 cameras). Coarse "person down" is solved-ish tech; nobody bridges from there to "needs wound care." [Hikvision](https://www.hikvision.com/en/core-technologies/see-smarter-technology/fall-detection/) · [CVEDIA](https://www.cvedia.com/accident-fall-detector) · [Videonetics](https://www.videonetics.com/ai-enable-video) · [AP CCTV360](https://www.cioandleader.com/videonetics-powers-andhra-pradeshs-statewide-ai-led-cctv360-real-time-governance-system-one-of-indias-largest-public-safety-and-smart-mobility-surveillance-network/)

**Academic work** maps encampments from street-view archives and satellites (LA tent monitoring with GroundingDINO, arXiv Aug 2025; encampment counting; TentNet) — all municipal monitoring framing, all avoiding person-level detection for ethics/accuracy reasons. [arXiv:2508.06409](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.06409) · [survey arXiv:2503.11727](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.11727)

**Inferring need for medical supplies from street imagery: nothing exists, anywhere.** Multiple targeted searches found no product, pilot, or paper. The nearest medical-AI adjacency is **Akido Labs' ScopeAI** — AI assisting street-medicine *clinicians* treating homeless patients in LA (CalMatters, Jan 2026) — carried by the clinician, not watching from a pole. Wound-assessment CV exists only in consented clinical settings (smartphone wound monitoring, responder AR tools). This is white space — and San Jose is the demonstration of what occupying it costs. [Akido](https://calmatters.org/housing/homelessness/2026/01/akido-street-medicine-ai/)

**India camera reality.** ITMS enforcement cameras (ANPR, red-light, speed) use IR-corrected lenses angled at number plates and stop lines — evidence packets are vehicle image + plate crop; pedestrians and sidewalks are incidental. Operators are state/city traffic police TMCs and Smart Cities Mission SPV ICCCs (all 100 cities operational since Aug 2023), built by integrators (Honeywell, L&T, BEL). Feeds are contractually locked — Bengaluru's Safe City contract sets a **₹5 lakh penalty per unauthorized feed access**. Documented third-party access exists only as government contracts/MoUs: Staqu analyzing UP prison feeds for the state; an IISc–Bengaluru Traffic Police research MoU; and **IUDX** (MoHUA/IISc India Urban Data Exchange), which offers API-based ICCC integration including smart-pole video with ~7-day retention — the one institutional door that could someday matter. No startup has general access to live traffic feeds. [RLVDS specs](https://www.i2vsys.com/assets/files/i2V_ITMS_2022.pdf) · [PIB ICCC](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1947455) · [Citizen Matters on Safe City](https://citizenmatters.in/safe-city-project-cctv-cameras-surveillance-crime-against-women/) · [Staqu](https://inc42.com/buzz/gurugram-startup-staqu-develops-ai-surveillance-system-for-uttar-pradesh-prisons/) · [IUDX](https://iudx.org.in/cities/)

## 2. Fulfilment and funding rails

**Direct precedent for gig riders delivering aid to a homeless person at a street pin: none found, any geography.** The closest rails all stop at a fixed address or an organization's door:

- **DoorDash Project DASH** (US/Canada, 2018–active): nonprofits become senders on DoorDash logistics; **Dashers are paid normal rates**; 8M+ deliveries / 125M+ meals. The proven "CSR pays, gig rider delivers aid" template — to addresses, never street pins. [Project DASH](https://about.doordash.com/en-us/impact/project-dash)
- **Glovo Access** (Spain/global): 2M+ meals, 480K+ social-purpose orders — to vulnerable people's *doors*. [Glovo](https://about.glovoapp.com/press/glovo-delivers-2-million-meals-to-vulnerable-people-in-global-effort-to-reduce-food-waste/)
- **Zomato Feeding India**: 200K+ meals/day across 39 cities at ~₹10/meal via 145+ partner NGOs at fixed feeding sites — funded largely by **₹1–10 checkout donations in the Zomato and Blinkit apps** (a ready-made funding rail for kits). [feedingindia.org](https://www.feedingindia.org/)
- **Swiggy Serves** (Jan 2025): restaurants flag surplus; **Robin Hood Army volunteers** do the last mile. RHA itself: 275K+ volunteers, 162M meals, 407 cities — and an explicit **zero-funds policy** that blocks CSR-funded kit schemes through them. [Swiggy blog](https://blog.swiggy.com/swiggy-catalyst/swiggy-and-robin-hood-army-join-hands-to-bring-the-swiggy-serves-programme-to-life/) · [RHA](https://robinhoodarmy.com/)
- **Blinkit 10-minute ambulance**: launched Jan 2025 in Gurugram; by Jan 2026, **25 BLS ambulances** covering Gurugram/Manesar/parts of NCR, 4,200+ cases, ~₹2,000 flat, stated aim of all major cities in ~2 years — praised by an AIIMS professor, publicly cautioned on compliance by the Commerce Minister. The strongest proof that Indian quick-commerce will run street-level medical logistics under scrutiny. [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/start-ups/blinkit-expands-10-minute-ambulance-service-125072401442_1.html) · [1-year mark](https://startuppedia.in/trending/blinkit-ambulance-services-turns-1-with-25-units-ceo-albinder-says-it-was-uncomfortable-territory-grateful-to-the-teams-on-the-ground-10969581)
- **Uber**: Direct×Albertsons surplus-food logistics (US, org recipients); Feeding America (Uber Health); India×RHA free rides for volunteers (COVID era). Pattern across all platforms: **they donate money, tech, and rides — the human last mile to street homeless is always volunteers or government, never paid riders.**
- **Zepto**: nothing relevant. **Dunzo**: shut down Jan 2025.

**The no-address problem.** [Addressing the Unaddressed](https://www.addressingtheunaddressed.org/) gave ~200K Kolkata slum dwellings Google Plus Code door plates by 2021, unlocking Swiggy/Zomato/Amazon and bank KYC ([MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2021/04/28/1022940/slum-dwellers-india-google-plus-code-access-delivery-apps/)) — but that addresses *dwellings*. For rough sleepers the real blocker is phone-lessness (delivery UX assumes call/OTP on arrival); the working Indian pattern is **reporter-as-proxy** (below).

## 3. Human reporting and street outreach — the working alternative to cameras

- **StreetLink** (England/Wales, 2012–): public reports a rough sleeper → routed to 300+ local outreach providers. Sobering outcomes from ~170k referrals (2012–19): **only 23% resulted in the person being found; ~5% led to housing; 36% never located** (Crisis 2018 evaluation). The government killed the mobile app in Sept 2023 (website remains). The reference model *and* the cautionary tale: reports are cheap; **follow-through is the product**. [Crisis evaluation (PDF)](https://www.crisis.org.uk/media/238711/evaluation_of_the_streetlink_project_2018.pdf) · [app scrapped](https://www.swlondoner.co.uk/uncategorised/16102023-government-scraps-streetlink)
- **Rain Basera app + helpline 14461** (DUSIB, Delhi): citizen photographs a person → GPS auto-captured → nearest rescue team dispatched; 24×7 control room; 15–16 GPS-tracked van teams patrol 10pm–4am in winter; 197 permanent shelters + ~250 winter tents. A real, functioning, government report→dispatch system — Delhi-only, and its goal is relocation to shelters, not serving people in place. [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dusib.rainbasera) · [winter plan](https://theprint.in/india/delhi-govt-launches-winter-action-plan-for-homeless/1264020/)
- **No Food Waste** (Coimbatore + 9 cities): citizens pin **"hunger spots"** with headcount in the app → NGO verifies → surplus food is delivered to the spot; 285k+ people fed. **The closest existing implementation anywhere of report → verify → physical delivery at a street location.** Food-only, spot-level. [Borgen](https://www.borgenmagazine.com/the-indian-ngo-using-food-recovery/)
- **Samaritan** (Seattle): Bluetooth beacons carried by *enrolled* homeless members; donors fund goods redeemed via nonprofits after case-worker check-ins; since pivoted toward healthcare-funded models (2024 LA clinic pilot reduced ED usage). The consent-based, enrollment-based opposite of cameras. [CHCF](https://www.chcf.org/resource/samaritan-developing-digital-tools-for-people-experiencing-homelessness-app-for-unhoused-people/)
- **Miracle Messages / Miracle Money** (US): reunification + $750/mo basic income; USC RCT with positive stability findings. Cash, not kits. [USC study](https://dworakpeck.usc.edu/news/guaranteed-income-study-finds-meaningful-results-for-people-experiencing-homelessness-cash)
- Graveyard: **HandUp** (wound down), **WeShelter** (dormant), **Concrn** (crisis-responder dispatch, ceased ~2019). **Beam** (UK) alive with council contracts, mixed record.
- **ML belongs on reports, not imagery**: DSSG × Homeless Link built triage/prioritization ML over StreetLink citizen reports (2020) — the defensible place for AI in this space. [DSSG](https://dssgfellowship.org/project/homeless-link/)
- **Street medicine**: Street Medicine Institute programs span 140+ cities in 27 countries with wound-care protocols. India: a 2024 scoping review records Delhi street-medicine consults with 16,000+ individuals including wound care ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11204218/)); BUDS mobile health van (Delhi NCR), Smile Foundation "Smile on Wheels", IHBAS mobile mental-health unit, Doctors For You. The medical part of your story already has owners — they lack routing signal and supplies, not mission.

## 4. Goonj and Indian government programs

**Goonj** (Delhi, since 1999; Anshu Gupta): 3,000+ tons/yr of urban surplus flows through sorting/processing centers to grassroots partners, where communities **earn** kits through development work ("Cloth for Work"/"Dignity for Work", 1,500+ projects) — the founding ethos is literally **"charity kills dignity."** Rahat disaster kits; "Not Just a Piece of Cloth" (4M+ cloth pads); winter **"Odha Do Zindagi"** blanket/sleeping-bag drives are the main street-homeless touchpoint. Urban homeless are *not* the core beneficiary; no tech or delivery-platform partnership exists. Implication: Goonj is an excellent kit-assembly partner *if* the model is framed around dignity and participation — anonymous camera-targeted charity drops are the opposite of their theory of change. [goonj.org/rahat](https://goonj.org/rahat/) · [profile](https://give.do/blog/goonj-an-idea-that-changed-millions-of-lives-in-india/)

**Government**: SC PUCL orders (2010) make shelter for the urban homeless a state obligation under Art 21 ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-shelter-for-homeless-pan-india-issue-union-asked-to-verify-statistics-submit-data-on-urban-poverty-alleviation-mission-283718)). The **SMILE scheme** (₹100 cr) funds beggary "identification, rescue, rehabilitation" — expanding July 2026 to make 295 cities "begging-free" — an institutional-rehab (and coercion-adjacent) model, not street aid. Bengaluru's 48 BBMP shelters have documented refusal problems (people prefer pavements where food/blankets arrive). [SMILE guidelines](https://smile-b.dosje.gov.in/public/guideline/smile-beggary-guidelines-2025.pdf) · [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/homeless-people-from-streets-of-bengaluru-refuse-night-shelters-claim-they-are-poorly-maintained-3850289)

## 5. Legal and ethics

**"Where the homeless are" data gets reused against them — documented, including in India.**
- US: HMIS data shared with 100+ orgs; ACLU sued Phoenix over sweeps (2022); a 2025 federal EO expanding data collection on unhoused people drew condemnation; Clearview AI's patent names identifying "homeless people" as a use case. [Tech Policy Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/harnessing-ai-use-it-to-stabilize-not-stigmatize-homelessness/) · [ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/aclu-condemns-trump-executive-order-targeting-disabled-and-unhoused-people)
- India: pre-G20 2023 Delhi demolitions — DUSIB razed 8 of its *own* shelters, activists claim ~3 lakh people affected ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/india-g20-delhi-homeless-shelters-1.6957828)); Hyderabad rounded up beggars before a 2017 VIP visit and recorded biometrics/Aadhaar ([BBC](https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/world-asia-india-41965174)); **Indore, from Jan 2025: FIRs against people *giving* alms** (up to 1 yr / ₹5,000), CCTV monitoring of intersections, ₹1,000 informer rewards — run under the Union SMILE scheme ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/madhya-pradesh/from-january-1-fir-to-be-lodged-against-people-giving-alms-to-beggars-in-indore-3319438)). Begging remains criminal in ~20 states/UTs (unverified count); the Delhi HC's 2018 decriminalization (Harsh Mander) applies to Delhi only. [Judgment](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/117834652/)

**DPDP Act 2023**: Rules notified Nov 2025; full obligations by May 2027. CCTV images of identifiable people are personal data; strangers on streets cannot practicably consent. The s7 "legitimate uses" carve-out (medical emergency / threat to life / disaster) is the strongest arguable basis — framed for genuine emergencies, not continuous scanning (untested). No sensitive-data tier (health inference is legally under-regulated but ethically radioactive); government bodies can be exempted under s17 while a startup is not. No CCTV-specific statute; no published ICCC data-sharing framework. [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/11/223-dpdp-act-citizens-cctv-surveillance/)

**Medical/regulatory boundary**: India regulates software as a medical device under the Medical Device Rules 2017; CDSCO's draft Medical Device Software guidance (Oct 2025) adds AI/ML classes — image-based "needs medical attention" flagging is plausibly licensable. The US FDA (CDS guidance, updated Jan 2026) and EU (MDR Rule 11; AI Act Annex III high-risk for triage/dispatch; Art 5 bans on biometric categorization inferring sensitive attributes, in force Feb 2025) converge on treating this function as regulated. Delivering medicines implicates the Drugs & Cosmetics Act (e-pharmacy rules still un-notified) → **kits must stay OTC/first-aid**. India's Good Samaritan protection (SC 2016; MV Act §134A) covers good-faith bystanders at **road accidents** — not organized dispatch by a company. [CAM on CDSCO](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2026/01/medical-device-as-software-has-cdsco-guidance-changed-the-rules/) · [EU AI Act Art 5](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/5/)

**Gig-worker law**: Rajasthan's 2023 act (first, implementation lagging) and Karnataka's act (in force May 2025) — 1–5% welfare fee per transaction, contracts must be exhaustive with **14-day notice of any change**, algorithmic transparency on task assignment/pay, safety and grievance duties. Routing riders to "aid drops" is a regulated contract/algorithm change, landing amid live union mobilization (Feb 2025 Karnataka demands to withdraw 10-minute deliveries on safety grounds). [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/08/223-karnataka-gig-worker-law-algorithmic-transparency-gaps/)

**Supportive facts**: DPDP s7 emergency basis exists; full DPDP enforcement only by May 2027 (runway); shelter is declared state policy (PUCL); Good Samaritan protects individual helpers within scope; officials have been receptive to service-connection framing (San Jose's mayor) and to delivery-app health experiments (AIIMS praise for Blinkit's ambulance).

## 6. Open source and datasets

**GitHub is essentially empty.** "Homeless detection" surfaces only 0–1-star toy/class projects (e.g., [EncampmentVision](https://github.com/pritkc/EncampmentVision) — Street View download scripts, no model; an empty "VisionAid" repo). No repo above 3 stars. Academic code leads OSS and serves municipal monitoring, not aid. Fall detection's canonical repo ([Human-Falling-Detect-Tracks](https://github.com/GajuuzZ/Human-Falling-Detect-Tracks), 858★) is unmaintained research code; datasets are staged and indoor (UR Fall, UP-Fall), and UCF-Crime's 13 anomaly classes include no "person down/medical distress" — **no public street-level person-down dataset exists.** Hackathon projects (home++, Hack for the Homeless series) universally died. The most active OSS in the space is services-directory/case-management ([hackforla/HomeUniteUs](https://github.com/hackforla/HomeUniteUs), 44★). **No OSS links detection or reporting to physical fulfilment.**

## 7. Confirmed absences — where the idea is genuinely novel

Each of these was searched for directly and not found:

1. Any system inferring need for medical supplies / wound care from street, traffic, or CCTV imagery — no product, pilot, or paper.
2. Any program where delivery-platform gig riders bring aid to homeless individuals at street GPS pins — any geography.
3. Any app where a citizen report triggers *supplies* to a specific person (nearest: No Food Waste's spot-level food delivery; StreetLink dispatches humans; Samaritan requires enrollment).
4. Any Goonj × delivery-platform or tech-logistics partnership.
5. Any open-source project connecting street-level need signals to fulfilment.
6. Any published framework for third-party access to Indian ICCC/traffic camera feeds (and no startup precedent for live-feed access outside government contracts).
7. Any public street-level "person collapsed" dataset.

## 8. What the landscape teaches

1. **Object detection works; inferring human circumstance doesn't.** The same San Jose pipeline: 97% potholes, 10–15% lived-in cars. "Needs antiseptic" is far beyond "lived-in car."
2. **Rescue framing survives; monitoring framing dies.** Seoul (99% survival, celebrated) vs San Jose (component removed) vs Lucknow (discredited). Any acceptable CV is: narrow emergency class → human confirms → human responds.
3. **Platforms fund and build tech; the last mile to street homeless is always volunteers or government.** Paid riders serving unhoused people at pins is novel — and rider consent/training/safety is the unsolved hard part, under gig-worker laws that now regulate exactly this kind of task change.
4. **Reports are cheap; follow-through is the product.** StreetLink generated 170k reports and found the person only 23% of the time. Verification and outcome-tracking are where systems die.
5. **The working Indian pattern is reporter-as-proxy**: Rain Basera, helpline 14461, No Food Waste's hunger spots. Build on it rather than around it.
6. **In India, location data about poor people has a documented enforcement afterlife** (G20 demolitions, Hyderabad biometrics, Indore's camera-backed begging crackdown). Design so the dangerous database never exists: ephemeral pins, no identity capture, no face storage, aggregate-only analytics.
